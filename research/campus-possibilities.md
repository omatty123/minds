# Campus possibilities — 10 October 2026

Scope: 28 selected works, objects and teaching adaptations. This is a recruitment and planning collection, not an exhaustive departmental inventory or an adopted syllabus. `possibilities.json` is the complete candidate inventory; each record includes source, activity, materials, output, estimated time and contextual limits. Activities and timings are editorial proposals, not classroom-tested protocols. None claims a faculty or departmental endorsement.

## Campus coverage

The full public Lux collections directory at https://lux.lawrence.edu/communities.html was examined, including academic collections, Conservatory, library/archives, galleries, student life and community engagement. It is an archival collections directory, not a current organizational chart. The current academic landing page and campus navigation were also consulted; its complete paginated program catalog was not retrieved. Coverage therefore means broad possible connections, not verified representation of every current program. A campus collection activity explicitly still needs three actual objects selected with a librarian or archivist.

## Source depth

Existing named works reuse the source research in the learning bank and global history; see their review-depth fields. New sources include the original Stroop article (full text); Vickhoff et al. on musical structure, breathing and heart-rate variability (full text); Roediger and Karpicke on test-enhanced learning (abstract); Konvalinka et al. on fire-walking synchrony (indexed abstract, with a PMC access challenge); Tversky and Kahneman on framing (indexed original-paper excerpt); Reich's composer notes; UNESCO's indexed Vedic chanting description; Smithsonian Folkways' indexed album description; RCSB PDB-101's acetylcholine receptor account; the Exploratorium blind-spot procedure; and selected opening passages of James's chapter XI on attention.

The chant and San healing-dance entries are listening/context activities with specialist collaboration proposed. They are not instructions to reproduce a sacred practice or induce trance. The simple group humming activity uses neutral sound and does not claim to replicate the physiological experiment. Fire-walking is a published-figure analysis, not a reenactment. Source recordings, scores, complete editions and permissions must be arranged for actual teaching; media playback and rights clearance were not established here. No recordings were downloaded or substituted with synthetic voices.

## Interaction and maintenance

Four suggested routes select four candidates each. Filters combine search, campus connection, activity and time. Shortlist IDs and filters persist in the URL, not a personal account. Print-shortlist exposes selected records and their teaching notes. All candidates and source disclosures remain available without JavaScript. The build copies the source JSON unchanged to the public directory.
